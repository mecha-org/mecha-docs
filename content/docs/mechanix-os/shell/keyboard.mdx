# Keyboard

Mechanix OS features a custom-built, in-house virtual keyboard designed specifically to meet the unique requirements of our embedded devices and global user base.

## Why In-House?

Existing open-source keyboard solutions often struggled to meet our specific needs for performance on embedded hardware and deep system integration. By developing our own solution, we achieved:

- **Localization & Multi-language Support**: A primary design goal was to support a wide array of languages out of the box, ensuring Mechanix OS is accessible globally.
- **Embedded Optimization**: The keyboard is lightweight and highly optimized for touch interactions on small-to-medium screens, ensuring low latency and high responsiveness even on constrained hardware.
- **Deep Customization**: It routes input through our specific windowing system and pairs seamlessly with our theming engine.

## Hardware Integration

The keyboard system is not just virtual; it is designed to bridge the gap between touch and physical input.

### Pogo Pin Extension
The Mecha Comet device features a pogo pin connector that supports a physical QWERTY keyboard attachment.
- **Seamless Switching**: The system detects when the physical keyboard extension is attached and automatically adjusts the UI (hiding the on-screen keyboard) to maximize screen real estate.
- **Unified Input Logic**: Both virtual and physical inputs are handled through a unified input stack, ensuring that shortcuts, layouts, and input methods remain consistent regardless of the input source.
