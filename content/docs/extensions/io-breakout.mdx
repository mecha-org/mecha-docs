## Overview

The **IO Breakout** is an expansion device designed to expose the Comet’s internal IO in a flexible way.

It features **40 pogo pins**, enabling seamless connection to external peripherals such as GPIO modules, keyboards and other custom peripherals.

## Hardware Specifications

- **Primary Interface**: 40-pin pogo connector
- **Expansion Type**: GPIO breakout and debug interface
- **Header Pitch**: 2.54 mm female headers (breadboard-compatible)
- **USB-to-UART IC**: [CP2102](https://www.silabs.com/interface/usb-bridges/classic/device.cp2102)
- **Intended Use**:
  - Prototyping and experimentation
  - Firmware flashing and debugging
  - External peripheral integration (keyboards, gamepads, sensors)

## Architecture

The **GPIO Extension** adapts the raw pogo-pin interface into standard, breadboard-compatible connectors while also providing a convenient communication interface to the host system. It converts the 40 pogo pins into **2.54 mm pitch female headers**, allowing easy access to all exposed signals using standard jumpers.

This makes it ideal for:

- Rapid prototyping
- Debugging and signal probing
- Connecting external sensors or modules

The IO Breakout is designed to bridge the gap between compact hardware design and open-ended experimentation by exposing internal signals through pogo pins and adapting them to familiar connectors without compromising the integrity of the core system.
